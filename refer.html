Design org help for your portfolio companies

I help early and growth-stage companies build their design orgs: who to hire, how to structure the team, how to fix a culture that is fraying, and how to make all of it stick.

who this page is for

Investors, advisors, and operators with founders in their orbit. It exists so you know exactly when an intro to me is useful, and what happens after you make one.

when to send a founder my way
01
They just raised, and design can't keep pace with product and engineering.
02
They're making their first design leadership hire, and nobody on the team can evaluate the candidates.
03
The founding designer is drowning, and the CEO can't tell whether they need a manager, more makers, or both.
04
Designers keep leaving, or they've gone quiet in critiques, and nobody can say why.
05
They're shipping an AI product and users don't trust it.
what I do about it

The core engagement is a design org audit and hiring plan, four to six weeks.

Where design sits today, what the next twelve months require, role profiles and interview loops the founder can run themselves, and the onboarding and critique foundations that make the hires stick. When the situation calls for it, I also step in as interim design leadership or advise on AI product design.

why founders take the call

I spent 25 years leading design, most recently running a fifteen-person global org at Intuit, where I built more than ten design teams across four countries. I've written up how I do this work: four org rebuilds in five years, each fully staffed in two quarters, delivery held or accelerated through every one. Read it here: Leading teams through change.

The failure patterns are the same at five designers and fifty. My job is to give a company only the structure its stage requires and nothing more.
