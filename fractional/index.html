who i help

Founders and product leaders at early and growth-stage companies who need senior design leadership but aren’t ready for a full-time hire.

Design org audit + hiring plan

core offer · 4 to 6 weeks

I assess your design capability and give you a plan you can act on.

where design sits today

Team skills, craft bar, how design partners with product and engineering, and where the gaps are.

what the next 12 months require

Team structure, seniority mix, and whether you need a leader, more makers, or both.

a hiring plan you can run

Role profiles, interview loops, portfolio review criteria, and what a strong candidate actually looks like at your stage.

foundations that make hires stick

Onboarding, critique and review rituals, and how design decisions get made.

This is work I’ve done repeatedly, not theoretically. At Intuit I built more than 10 design teams over my tenure: hiring from scratch, relocating teams across sites, onboarding designers into complex domains, and building team culture across Canada, the US, the UK, and India. I know what breaks when teams scale and how to set up a team so it doesn’t.

also available for

Interim design leadership

I step in while you hire, or before you’re ready to. I lead the team, raise the bar on craft, and hand off a healthy org. Typically 2-3 days per week, 3 to 6 months. Often a natural follow-on from an audit.

AI product design advisory

For teams shipping AI products: how the agent earns trust, what it asks, when it defers to a human, and how confidence is communicated. I directed the design vision for Intuit’s first AI tax agent.

how engagements start

A 30-minute conversation about what you’re trying to do. If there’s a fit, I’ll propose a scope. No pitch deck.
